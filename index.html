About Us

PurrKatty is a small family cattery. We breed a few litters a year, raise every kitten in the house rather than in pens, and only let them go to their new homes once they're vaccinated, microchipped and used to everyday household noise. Alongside the cattery we run a grooming room for cats of every breed, with the same calm, unhurried handling.

Our Services

  • Full Grooming

    A full groom from nose to tail: brushing out, bath, blow-dry, nail trim and ear clean, done slowly so even nervous cats settle.

  • Bath & Dry

    A gentle bath with a shampoo chosen for your cat's coat, then a warm-air dry and a brush-through. Ideal between full grooms.

  • Styling

    Trims and tidy-ups for long-haired breeds, from a hygiene clip to a full lion cut, always finished by hand.

  • Medical Bath

    A soothing medicated bath for cats with dry skin or dandruff, using products your vet has recommended.
