How to Keep Cat’s Coat Glossy

  • Posted October 4, 2026 in Animal Care

  • 3 Comments
  • 3 Links

A glossy coat is one of the clearest signs of a healthy cat, and most of what makes it shine happens at home rather than at the groomer's. These are the habits we follow with our own cats and recommend to every family that takes one of our kittens.

Start with food. A complete, good-quality diet with enough protein and fatty acids does more for the coat than any shampoo. If your cat's coat turns dull or flaky, a change of food is often the first thing your vet will suggest.

Brush little and often. Two or three short sessions a week are enough for most short-haired cats.

A coat looks after itself when the cat does. Our job is mostly to keep her well fed, calm and brushed.

From Ichae Semos

Finally, keep an eye on water. Many cats drink too little, so a water fountain or a second bowl away from the food can help. And if the coat changes suddenly, with bald patches, scratching or a greasy feel, book a check with your vet rather than reaching for another product.
