Version 2.0 — 2026
------------------

A rebuild on a modern stack — the same design, checked block by block
against version 1 at desktop, tablet and phone widths — plus the pages the
site always pointed at but never had, a booking-confirmation email in the
same design, and the things that were broken in version 1 quietly fixed.


NEW
- services.html — the six services (full grooming, bath & dry, styling,
  medical bath, V.I.P. service, boarding) with what each includes, the
  price and the time, and a Book This button on each.
- booking.html — the page behind "Request a Call" and the three packages'
  buttons: name, phone, email, the cat, a package or service (preselected
  from the button you came from), a date, the best time to call and notes,
  with how-it-works and the phone number beside it.
- shop.html and product.html — the shop behind the home page's four
  products: eight items with a category filter, and a product page with
  details, quantity and an order button, plus related items.
- 404.html — a not-found page with three ways back in.
- email-booking.html — the confirmation a customer receives after booking:
  the details in a box, add-to-calendar and change buttons, before-you-come
  notes, address and hours. Table-based, inline-styled, 600px wide, with an
  Outlook conditional and a phone layout — the way mail clients need it.
  Before sending, replace the images/... paths with absolute URLs on your
  server and put your links in place of the "#" ones (the file says so at
  the top).

FIXED (things that were broken in version 1)
- 113 "#" links now go somewhere: Read More to the about page, Request a
  Call and the packages' Contact Us to the booking page, the shop items to
  the product page, the gallery "link" icons to the gallery (and, on the
  gallery page, to the full-size photo), the post's tags and categories to
  the blog, the four social icons in every banner and the footer to the
  networks.
- The "Send Message" and "Post Comment" buttons were plain links that did
  nothing; they submit the form now, and the form checks its fields first.
- The Instagram strip on the home page ran against Instagram's old API
  (shut down in 2018) — with an access token shipped in the code — and
  showed nothing. It is a static "follow us" strip of nine photos linking
  to Instagram now, and the token is gone.
- The comment form asked for the website in an email field with a
  duplicate id; the search box had no label.
- The site name was an <h1> on every page (twice on the home page) and again
  in the footer. Each page has one h1 now — its block title, the post's title
  on the post, the first slide's tagline on the home page — with the same
  look.
- On a phone: the testimonial box cut off the quote (a fixed 300px box; it
  grows with the text now), the home banner was a thin 208px strip (it is a
  400px band with the photo filling it), and the shop went two-up between
  400 and 767px (one product per row now).

DESIGN
- Unchanged. The fonts are version 1's own Font Squirrel kit (Questrial and
  Baloo Thambi), self-hosted as before, woff2 only.

UNDER THE HOOD
- Bootstrap 3.3.6 + jQuery 3.2 + lightbox.js + instafeed → Bootstrap 5.3,
  no jQuery. js/main.js is one dependency-free file: the gallery lightbox,
  the menu closing after a tap, form validation, back-to-top, the shop
  filter. The vertical banner slider and the testimonial slider are
  Bootstrap's own carousel.
- Font Awesome 4 → 6 (woff2 subset).
- css/bs3-compat.css: the Bootstrap 3 behaviours this design relies on
  (the 1170 container, the media object, the carousel controls and caption,
  the input group) restored on top of Bootstrap 5. Safe to trim.
- Every page: a unique <title> and meta description, lang="en", one h1,
  no inline styles, no inline scripts, every image with alt text and
  width/height (no layout shift), lazy loading below the fold, labelled
  form fields, https links.
- home.html redirects to index.html (the entry page is index.html now).
